Clear boundaries

Helpful meal context, not a diagnosis.

Can BurnLoop manage my blood pressure?

No. BurnLoop provides general wellness and educational food guidance. It does not diagnose, treat, or manage high blood pressure.

What should I do with a sodium-related prompt?

Use it as an educational reminder while planning future meals. For specific sodium targets, medication, symptoms, or treatment changes, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
