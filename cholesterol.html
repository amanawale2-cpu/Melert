Clear boundaries

Support for food choices—not a treatment plan.

Can BurnLoop lower my cholesterol?

No. BurnLoop is general wellness support. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace personalized care from a qualified clinician.

How should I use a meal alert?

Use it as a helpful prompt for a future food decision. For individual nutrition targets, medicines, symptoms, or treatment changes, seek professional advice.
