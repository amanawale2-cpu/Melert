Frequently asked questions

Useful guidance, with clear boundaries.

Does BurnLoop provide medical advice?

No. BurnLoop provides general wellness and educational food guidance. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace a qualified healthcare professional.

How does BurnLoop personalize guidance?

You can share your goals, preferences, allergies, and relevant health considerations. BurnLoop uses that context to make guidance more relevant to you.

What do the daily signals mean?

Energy, Sugar Spike, and Fullness are designed to give you an easy-to-read view of how your day is trending and help you choose a practical next step.

Can I scan a meal?

BurnLoop is designed to reduce meal-logging effort with meal scanning, so you can get guidance without turning every meal into data entry.
