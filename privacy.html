Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Melert (“Melert,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) provides personalized food and general wellness guidance through the Melert iOS app (the “App”). This Privacy Policy describes the information involved in using Melert, how it may be processed, and the choices available to you.

1. Information Involved in Using Melert

  • Account details. If you create an account or sign in, the App may process your email address, display name, sign-in identifiers, and account-related information through its authentication provider.
  • Profile and preferences. Information you choose to enter may include wellness goals, health considerations, allergies, dietary preferences, and other details used to personalize meal guidance. Do not enter information you do not want used for personalization.
  • Meal photos and meal details. When you scan or record a meal, the App processes the image you select or capture, identified or edited food names, portions, meal timestamps, and related meal history.
  • Meal reviews and personalized guidance. The App generates estimated meal-fit assessments and food-impact indicators, which may include sugar-spike, sodium-stress, energy, and fullness-related guidance. These are estimates based on available inputs, not measurements of your body's actual response.
  • Check-ins and learned patterns. If you record that you ate a meal or provide follow-up feedback, Melert may store the check-in, your responses, and patterns inferred from your meal history to improve future guidance.
  • AI plans and coaching. Food preferences, profile details, meal history, and other relevant context may be used to prepare meal plans, coaching suggestions, and responses to requests you submit.
  • Apple Health data (optional). If you grant permission, Melert may read supported Apple Health information relevant to its features, such as activity or active-energy information. The App's access is limited to the data categories and permissions you approve through Apple Health. Apple Health access is optional.
  • Subscriptions and transactions. Apple and our subscription service provider may process product identifiers, entitlement status, transaction identifiers, and purchase information necessary to activate and restore subscriptions. Melert does not receive your full payment-card details from Apple.
  • Technical and support information. Depending on the services enabled in the App, technical diagnostics, app version, device or operating-system information, and information you include in support requests may be processed to maintain and troubleshoot the service.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide meal analysis, personalized food-fit estimates, meal check-ins, learned patterns, AI-generated plans, progress features, subscription access, account functions, customer support, security, troubleshooting, and service improvements. Personalization depends on the information you choose to provide and the permissions you grant.

3. AI Processing and Meal Photos

Melert uses third-party AI services, including Google Gemini, for features such as meal-photo interpretation, meal guidance, and plan generation. To fulfill a request, relevant content may be transmitted to the AI provider. Depending on the feature, this can include a meal image, food descriptions, selected profile preferences or health considerations, and relevant meal or feedback context.

Do not submit images, notes, or sensitive information you do not want processed for these purposes. Third-party providers process submitted information under their applicable terms and privacy practices. AI-generated results may be incomplete or inaccurate.

4. Apple Health and Device Permissions

Apple Health integration is optional. Melert requests access only through Apple's permission controls, and you can change those permissions in the Health app or iOS settings. Melert uses permitted Health information to support relevant activity, progress, or personalized wellness features.

Melert does not sell Apple Health information or use it for targeted advertising. Apple Health information is not required to use Melert's core meal-scanning features. Whether particular Health-derived values are included in an AI request depends on the feature and its implementation; review the permissions and data you choose to share.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Melert may send local reminders, including a follow-up check-in after you log a meal. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings. Notification timing and delivery can depend on device settings and iOS behavior.

6. Service Providers and Sharing

Melert uses service providers for functions such as authentication, subscription management, AI processing, and, where enabled, app diagnostics or analytics. These may include Apple, RevenueCat, Google Gemini, and Firebase services. Information is shared with a provider only as needed for the relevant feature or service, subject to its applicable processing arrangements.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to respond to lawful requests, or to protect the safety, rights, and security of users and services. Melert does not sell personal health information to advertisers.

7. Storage and Retention

Meal history, preferences, check-ins, and learned patterns may be stored on your device. Account, purchase, support, and other service-related records may be held by the providers involved in those functions. Content sent to an AI service is processed outside the device and may be retained by that provider according to its applicable policies and settings.

We retain information under our control for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service. Removing the App from your device does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription or erase records held by third-party providers.

8. Your Choices and Requests

  • Meal records: Use available in-app controls to manage or delete recorded meals and related history.
  • Apple Health: Review or revoke access in the Apple Health app's sharing and app-permission controls.
  • Camera and photos: Manage access in iOS Settings and choose which images to submit.
  • Notifications: Turn notifications on or off in iOS Settings.
  • Subscriptions: Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings under your Apple Account's Subscriptions section. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.
  • Access, correction, and deletion: Contact contact@melert.net to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we control. Some information may need to be retained for legal or transaction-related reasons; provider-managed records may be subject to separate retention rules.

9. Security and International Processing

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Information handled by service providers may be processed in countries other than your own, subject to applicable legal requirements.

10. Children

Melert is designed for general wellness use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us at contact@melert.net.

11. Health and Wellness Limitations

Melert offers informational wellness guidance, not medical diagnosis, treatment, or monitoring. Meal scores, predicted impacts, and AI suggestions are estimates and should not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional. This section describes the nature of the service and does not limit your privacy rights.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Melert's features, providers, or legal obligations change. The date at the top indicates the most recent revision. Where required, we will provide additional notice.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email contact@melert.net.
